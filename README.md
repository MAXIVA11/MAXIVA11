A protocol that carries its message entirely in silence. A 3D world generated
from nothing but a song's tempo and mood. A tool that exfiltrates data over
ping packets because why not. I like taking a weird idea seriously enough to
actually ship it, whether that idea is art or an attack surface.

### 🧪 Things I've built

- **[PulseTP](https://github.com/MAXIVA11/PulseTP)** (Go): a rhythm-based network protocol. The timing between UDP packets carries the message, not the packets themselves.
- **[Auralscape](https://github.com/MAXIVA11/Auralscape)** (JS): drop in a song and ride through a dusk-lit world built entirely from its own tempo, key, and mood.
- **[PingMeMaybe](https://github.com/MAXIVA11/PingMeMaybe)** (PowerShell): a covert data exfiltration channel over encrypted ICMP, for when the network itself is the attack surface.
- **[SecgresDB](https://github.com/MAXIVA11/SecgresDB)** (Python): scans PostgreSQL databases for sensitive data hiding in plain sight.
- **[The Cookie Jar](https://github.com/MAXIVA11/The_Cookie_Jar)** (JS): a small, honest look at how many cookies a site actually leaves on you.
- **[Xbox360Achievement](https://github.com/MAXIVA11/Xbox360Achievement)** (Python): a pixel-perfect retro Xbox 360 achievement popup, because nostalgia deserved better tooling.
